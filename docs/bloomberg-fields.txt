BLOOMBERG FIELDS REQUESTED BY THE TWO R JOBS
============================================

Source of truth is the R, not the Python port:

  PART A   no_git/CreateTradingDataENT.r   (676 lines)
  PART B   no_git/LimitUpDown.r            (515 lines)

Line numbers are into whichever file the part names.
"covered" means equity_master carries a column of that name.


THE ONE-LINE ANSWER
-------------------
TradingData can come off Bloomberg almost entirely.  LimitUpDown cannot
come off Bloomberg at all: PX_MAX_LIMIT and PX_MIN_LIMIT are the two
fields the whole job exists to produce, and equity_master has neither.
See PART B and the combined table at the end.


================================================================
PART A - CreateTradingDataENT.r
================================================================

There are four distinct Bloomberg surfaces in this job and they are not
equally removable:

  (a) EquitiesDataLicence.rds   a bulk file on disk, read at :83.  No
                               terminal, no rate limit.  Most of the
                               reference columns come from here.
  (b) R_bdp                     live terminal calls, at :103, :120, :181,
                                :273
  (c) load_BbgIntraday          intraday, at :357
  (d) load_FXdatas              FX, at :145.  A Sibyl call; probably not
                                Bloomberg, unverified.

equity_master replaces (a) and (b) together, because it IS the Data Licence
feed loaded into kdb.

A3. R_bdp AT :120                       (DEAD CODE - see the :113 bug)

   FIELD                  FEEDS                     COVERED
   CUR_MKT_CAP            top-up                    yes
   MKT_CAP_LAST_TRD       top-up                    no
   EQY_BETA               top-up                    yes
   BETA_ADJ_OVERRIDABLE   top-up                    no
   INTERVAL_VOLATILITY    top-up                    no
   INDUSTRY_SECTOR        top-up                    yes

   :113 reads `if (length(idx) == 0)`, where idx is the set of rows MISSING
   those fields.  The block therefore runs only when there is nothing to
   fix, on an empty frame.  MKT_CAP_LAST_TRD, BETA_ADJ_OVERRIDABLE and
   INTERVAL_VOLATILITY have never populated anything.  Almost certainly
   should be `> 0`.  Preserved verbatim; not silently fixed.  The Python
   run reports how many rows WOULD have entered the top-up, which is the
   number that says whether fixing it in R would change anything.

================================================================
PART B - LimitUpDown.r
================================================================

One surface only.  Every field comes from R_bdp - live terminal calls,
four call sites, no RDS file, no intraday, no FX.  Securities are always
addressed as `<BloombergCode> Equity` (built at :427 and :390).


B1. R_bdp AT :189                       (the main call, every market
                                         except Indonesia)

   FIELD                  FEEDS                     COVERED
   PX_MAX_LIMIT           LimitUpPrice              NO
   PX_MIN_LIMIT           LimitDownPrice            NO
   MARKET_STATUS          ACTV filter, :201 :250    yes
   PX_LAST                the sanity check at :252  yes

   :252 flags any name whose PX_LAST falls outside its own returned band
   and emails 'WrongLimitBBG'.  PX_LAST is not written to the output.

   Not a field, but it decides who gets requested: :180 drops Thai
   foreign-board lines (SET-MAIN with /F or /Q in the BloombergCode)
   before the call.


B2. R_bdp AT :212                       (the retry, up to 2 extra passes,
                                         only for names that came back
                                         with a null PX_MAX_LIMIT)

   FIELD                  FEEDS                     COVERED
   PX_MAX_LIMIT           LimitUpPrice              NO
   PX_MIN_LIMIT           LimitDownPrice            NO
   PX_LAST                carried through           yes
   VOLUME_AVG_5D          retry filter at :222      NO - equity_master has
                                                    PX_VOLUME, one day, not
                                                    a 5-day average
   ID_MIC_PRIM_EXCH       retry filter at :222      yes
   MARKET_STATUS          retry filter at :222      yes

   :222 keeps only VOLUME_AVG_5D > 0 AND ID_MIC_PRIM_EXCH != "ROCO" AND
   MARKET_STATUS == "ACTV".  ROCO is the Taiwan emerging-stock board, an
   unsupported segment.  The two filter fields exist purely to decide
   which no-limit names are worth complaining about - a name that fails
   them is dropped quietly instead of emailing 'NoLimitBBG'.


B3. R_bdp AT :269                       (Indonesia only, JKT-MAIN, split
                                         off at :434)

   FIELD                  FEEDS                     COVERED
   PX_YEST_CLOSE          the base price for the    NO - equity_master has
                          whole threshold ladder,   px_last_prev, which is
                          :289 and :315             a PLAUSIBLE but
                                                    UNVERIFIED equivalent
   SECURITY_TYP           the filter at :327        yes
   MARKET_STATUS          requested, never read     yes

   Indonesia gets no PX_MAX_LIMIT / PX_MIN_LIMIT from Bloomberg at all.
   The limits are computed locally from PX_YEST_CLOSE against a percent
   ladder in the threshold CSV, floored at IDR 50 (:292).  :327 then drops
   anything whose SECURITY_TYP is "right" (case-insensitive) and anything
   left with a null LimitUpPrice.

   :335-382 is an older commented-out version of this same function.  It
   requests the identical three fields, so it adds nothing to this list.


B4. R_bdp AT :393                       (BSE secondary venue, India)

   FIELD                  FEEDS                     COVERED
   PX_MAX_LIMIT           LimitUpPrice              NO
   PX_MIN_LIMIT           LimitDownPrice            NO
   MARKET_STATUS          returned, kept in frame   yes

   Runs only for crosscode rows whose VenueList contains BSE-SECONDARY and
   whose BSEBloombergCode is not already a primary BloombergCode (:438-440).
   No retry loop and no PX_LAST sanity check on this path.


NOT A BLOOMBERG FIELD, DESPITE THE NAME
   BloombergStatus (:424, :156) is a column of the CrossCode file, not
   something requested from Bloomberg.  Same for BloombergCode,
   BSEBloombergCode and BSERic.  Don't go looking for them in equity_master.

Eight distinct fields:      PX_MAX_LIMIT, PX_MIN_LIMIT, MARKET_STATUS,
                            PX_LAST, VOLUME_AVG_5D, ID_MIC_PRIM_EXCH,
                            PX_YEST_CLOSE, SECURITY_TYP
Covered by equity_master:   MARKET_STATUS, PX_LAST, ID_MIC_PRIM_EXCH,

The four covered fields are all filters and checks.  The two that carry
the answer are the two that are missing.  So equity_master does not
remove Bloomberg from this job the way it does from TradingData - it
would at best trim the retry pass and the Indonesia call.

THE QUESTIONS THAT WOULD CLOSE THIS OUT
---------------------------------------
1. Is equity_master.volatility the 10-day figure?           (A)
2. Is fx_last a local->USD rate, matching load_FXdatas?     (A)
3. Does equity_master carry index-level rows (MXAU Index and
   friends), or only equities?  That decides whether the
   :186 MSCI validation can run.                            (A)
4. Is equity_master.px_last_prev the previous close, i.e.
   PX_YEST_CLOSE?  That is the whole Indonesia calculation.  (B)
5. Is there ANY source for the limit prices other than the
   terminal - a daily exchange file, a kdb table, anything?
   Without one, LimitUpDown keeps its BBG dependency.        (B)

A sixth, if anyone knows: is there any source at all for
GICS_SECTOR_NAME?  Without it Sector takes the fallback for every row and
three rungs of the MSCI ladder stay dead.
